# Product Context — LeBoisDirect

## Platform & Stack

- **Platform:** web
- **Framework:** Next.js 15 (App Router)
- **Language:** TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Motion:** GSAP + ScrollTrigger

## Product

**LeBoisDirect** est un site e-commerce premium français spécialisé dans la vente en ligne de bois de chauffage.

### Purpose
Permettre aux particuliers français de commander facilement du bois de chauffage de qualité avec livraison à domicile.

### Target Audience
- Propriétaires de maisons individuelles en France
- Utilisateurs de poêles, cheminées et inserts
- Recherche de commodité et qualité pour le chauffage hivernal
- Budget moyen à premium

### Core Value Proposition
- Bois de chauffage premium (chêne, hêtre, charme)
- Commande simple en ligne
- Livraison rapide à domicile
- Qualité contrôlée et séchage optimal

## User Journeys

### Primary: Purchase wood
1. Arrive on homepage → understand offering
2. Browse products or filter by category
3. Select product → view details
4. Choose variant (length, volume)
5. Add to cart
6. Proceed to checkout
7. Enter delivery info
8. Payment
9. Order confirmation

### Secondary: Learn
- Understand delivery process
- Learn about wood types and selection
- Discover brand story
- Read FAQ

## Key Surfaces

### Homepage (Persuade)
- Cinematic hero with brand photography
- Category navigation
- Featured products showcase
- 4-scene storytelling (Bois → Produit → Livraison → Chaleur)
- Quality reassurance
- Delivery information
- Product education
- Winter lifestyle
- FAQ preview

### Shop (/boutique) (Operate)
- Product grid (4 cols desktop, 2 mobile)
- Filters: category, species, length, price
- Sorting options
- Product cards with hover states

### Product Detail (/produit/[slug]) (Operate)
- Large image gallery
- Product specs
- Variant selection
- Quantity picker
- Add to cart
- Delivery info
- Detailed descriptions

### Cart & Checkout (Operate)
- Mini cart drawer from header
- Full cart page
- Multi-step checkout
- Calm, trustworthy interface

### Brand Pages (Read/Persuade)
- Notre Histoire (brand story)
- Livraison (delivery info)
- FAQ
- Legal pages

## Brand Personality

- **Contemporary, not rustic:** Clean, editorial aesthetic
- **Warm, not corporate:** Premium but accessible
- **Precise, not complicated:** Clear information hierarchy
- **French, not generic:** Cultural context and language
- **Editorial, not promotional:** Restrained visual storytelling

## Constraints

- Must feel premium but remain highly usable
- Cinematic motion on discovery surfaces
- Calm interface on conversion surfaces
- Real product imagery (lifestyle shots provided)
- No fake social proof, reviews, or claims
- Mobile-first responsive behavior
- Accessibility compliant
- Performance-conscious (lazy loading, optimized images)

## Success Metrics (Intent)

- Clear product presentation
- Smooth conversion flow
- Brand trust and memorability
- Efficient product discovery
- Professional delivery perception
